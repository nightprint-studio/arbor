# Why the assistant is called Imber

Internal note, not user-facing. The name appears in the assistant panel, its home screen, the system prompt
("You are Imber, the assistant built into Bennu…"), the docs and the palette entries ("Imber: …").

## The metaphor

Arbor is the suite: the forest. The products (Corvus, Sitta, Merula, Tyto, Picus, Bennu…) are the birds that live in
its trees, which is why they carry Latin bird names. The assistant is **not** one more bird, and it is not a product
of its own: it is what the whole forest runs on. Water. It reaches every tree, the trees take it up, the birds drink
from it. Answers also arrive as a stream, which makes the image hold technically as well.

*Imber* is the Latin word for **rain**. Latin keeps it in the same family as the other names of the suite
(Corvus, Sitta, Picus) while saying that it is a different kind of thing.

## Names considered and dropped

- **Nidus, Mycelium, Gemma, Dryas, Rowan, Ilex** — the "forest" family. Dropped because the assistant is not a thing
  that lives in the forest but what feeds it. Gemma is also the name of Google's open models.
- **Stilla** (Latin "drop"): liked, but an AI product with that name already exists and does almost the same job
  (a coding teammate working from Slack and GitHub), with a registered trademark and a large owner behind it.
- **Dew, Brook, Rill, Fons, Rivus** — the water family. Rill is a data company; the others were less distinctive.

## Checks made (not a legal clearance)

- Package names: `imber` does not exist on npm, PyPI or crates.io.
- GitHub: 165 repositories with "imber" in the name; none is an AI assistant or a developer tool with a following.
- About 1.9k web occurrences, mostly surnames, places (a village in Wiltshire) and unrelated projects.
- Close sound: **Imbue**, an AI company building coding agents. Judged acceptable.
- **Not done:** the trademark registers (EUIPO TMview, USPTO; classes 9 and 42). If the name ever leaves the project
  for a public release, check them first.
